## 当前外部核验与反证

### 带目的解锁

- 当前中国 App Store 页面：`别刷了喵` ¥1，页面明确“立约 → 限时 → 到点 → 复盘”，并有近期评论称“小红书使用时间明显变少”、以及“试过多款锁手机软件后认为其他产品功能冗余或收费高”。
  - https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id6807461021
- one sec 当前提供打开前 friction、custom intentions、re-intervention，并在 5.0 加入基于设备端模型的反思；其核心说明直接强调“只有真正有理由时才打开”。
  - https://apps.apple.com/us/app/one-sec-screen-time-focus/id1532875441
- ScreenZen 当前提供打开前等待、app goal、滚动中断和“Is this important”提示。
  - https://apps.apple.com/us/app/screenzen-screen-time-control/id1541027222
- Apple 技术约束：Family Controls 授权、Managed Settings shield、Device Activity 都是官方能力；App Store 分发前必须申请 Family Controls distribution entitlement，包含相关 Screen Time extension 时扩展也要分别申请。
  - https://developer.apple.com/documentation/familycontrols/authorizationcenter
  - https://developer.apple.com/documentation/managedsettings/shieldsettings
  - https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitymonitor
  - https://developer.apple.com/documentation/familycontrols/requesting-the-family-controls-entitlement
